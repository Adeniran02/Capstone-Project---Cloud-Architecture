# Course Project—Cloud Architecture

The purpose of this project was to assess the knowledge gained throughout the coursework and use it as a foundation to build a sustainable cloud architecture for a company, based on thorough research into business needs and desired outcomes.

In this article, I detail the entire process of designing a robust cloud architecture for **JustGo**, a global ride-hailing company transitioning from on-premises solutions to the cloud. The goal is to enhance the company's current data strategy to ensure scalability, optimization, efficiency, and cost-effectiveness. In any organization, data is crucial for addressing business challenges, improving customer satisfaction, developing advanced models to predict future outcomes, and strengthening marketing strategies. This process involves collaborating with various customer groups to identify the types of data that need to be collected, the methods for collecting and storing that data, and the processes it will undergo.
